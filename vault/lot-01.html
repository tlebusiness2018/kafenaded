Raleinn 1928 My friends are gone...

Watercolor on paper.

A contemporary figurative work engaging themes of impermanence, endurance, and psychological transition. Long vaulted and only recently presented, the piece reflects a sustained exploration of human presence at the edge of collapse and persistence.

A solitary figure emerges in partial abstraction, rendered through layered washes and drawn intervention. Gesture and erasure function together, allowing the image to exist between clarity and dissolution. Surrounding elements remain suggestive rather than descriptive, reinforcing a sense of temporal suspension.

Material condition and surface wear form an integral part of the work's meaning, emphasizing time, fragility, and the quiet resilience of image and medium alike.
